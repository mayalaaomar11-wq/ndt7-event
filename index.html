About the event

NDT from A to Z is our flagship technical event. Industry engineers walk you through the fundamentals of non-destructive testing, advanced inspection methods and their industrial applications, with specialized sessions and a hands-on workshop.

Fundamental methods
PT, MT, UT, RT and VT.
Advanced methods
Phased array, eddy current, X-ray.
Industry insights
Sessions with practicing engineers.
